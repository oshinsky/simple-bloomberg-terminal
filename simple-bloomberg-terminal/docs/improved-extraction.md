# Improved extraction

Extraction is intentionally evidence-first and narrow:

- COST and REVENUE discover named commercial counterparties.
- RISK discovers filing risks.
- Every result carries verbatim filing evidence.
- Financial tables and structured numeric feeds are outside the pipeline.
- Chat and measurement consume the same filing scan without sharing conversational contracts.

See [sec-extraction.md](sec-extraction.md) for the current implementation.
